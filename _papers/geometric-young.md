abstract: >-
  U.S. Import and Export Price Indexes are currently calculated with a modified
  Laspeyres formula at each level of aggregation. However, the geometric Young
  formula might be more appropriate for the first two levels of aggregation,
  which are the weight group and the classification group. If the geometric Young
  formula was used to calculate inflation from 2012 to 2022, then cumulative
  headline inflation would have decreased from 10.0 to 5.8 percent for imports
  and from 14.7 to 9.5 percent for exports. Half of this change comes from using
  the geometric Young formula at the weight-group level, and the other half comes
  from the classification-group level.
