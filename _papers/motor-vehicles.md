abstract: >-
  In this article, we estimate consumption of automobiles by using a user-cost
  approach and data from the Consumer Expenditure Surveys Interview Survey (CE).
  The user-cost approach is a method for valuing the flow of services from
  long-lived goods. A key input into user cost is the depreciation of the good,
  which we estimate by using purchase data from the CE.
